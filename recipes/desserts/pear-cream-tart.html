Pear cream tart recipe

Prep Time: 30 minutes - Cook Time: 40 minutes - Servings: 6

Ingredients:

  • 4 pears
  • pie crust (make it or buy it)
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 15 cl of heavy whipping cream
  • vanilla extract
  • a pinch of salt
  • (optional) cinnamon

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Line a tart dish with the crust and prick it with a fork.
  3. Bake for 5 minutes.
  4. Arrange the peeled, sliced pears on the crust.
  5. (optional) Sprinkle with cinnamon.
  6. Bake for 25 minutes.
  7. Pour the cream over the pears (egg yolk, whipping cream, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt, whisked together).
  8. Put the tart back in the oven, turn the oven off, and let the cream set for 5 minutes as the oven cools.

Notes:

  • Great with a scoop of vanilla ice cream (à la mode).
  • When using apples instead of pears, I sprinkle the tart with brown sugar before baking. Apples are less juicy than pears, so you can skip pre-baking the crust.
