Esoterica

Small, free web apps in the mystic arts. Each one runs in your browser with no sign-up: read the cards, cast the coins, draw the runes, play the tones, or draw the figures.

Sound and energy

Tones and vibrations, and the patterns they make: healing frequencies, singing bowls, sand dancing on a vibrating plate, the harmonies of the planets and the pendulum drawings of the harmonograph.

Healing Frequencies

Digital tuning forks: Solfeggio, chakras, organs, the Cosmic Octave and more, plus a chart of the whole frequency scale.

Tibetan Singing Bowls

Strike or rub seven virtual singing bowls, synthesized live with their beating overtones, and run a sound bath timer.

Cymatics

Play a frequency and watch the sand settle into its Chladni figure, on a plate or a drumhead.

Music of the Spheres

A live orrery where each planet sings as it orbits, rising and falling with its speed, as Kepler heard it.

Harmonograph Maker

Musical intervals drawn by pendulums: harmonograph spirals and Lissajous curves for the octave.

Sacred geometry

The shapes found in nature and in temples: the Flower of Life, the Platonic and Archimedean solids, the golden angle of the sunflower and the Sri Yantra.

Sacred Geometry

Draw and export the Vesica Piscis, Seed of Life, Flower of Life, Metatron's Cube and the Golden Spiral.

Platonic Solids

Turn the five Platonic solids in 3D, see their duals and measurements, and print nets to fold your own.

Archimedean Solids

All thirteen Archimedean solids in 3D, with their Catalan duals, measurements and printable nets.

Sunflower Golden Angle

Grow a sunflower from the golden angle, nudge it a tenth of a degree to see spokes appear, and count its Fibonacci spirals.

Sri Yantra

The nine interlocking triangles with lotus petals and gates, drawn to scale, colorable and exportable as SVG or PNG.

Sacred art

Designs drawn as a practice, by hand and often in a single line: mandalas, labyrinths, the Zen circle, the kolams of South India, Celtic knotwork, Islamic star patterns and Gothic rose windows.

Mandala Maker

Draw with mirrored symmetry, choose from 2 to 24 folds, and save your mandala as a PNG or an SVG.

Kolam Maker

South Indian dot-grid designs where a single unbroken line loops around every dot. Watch it being drawn, in one stroke.

Rose Window Maker

Build Gothic tracery with compass and straightedge, step by step, like a medieval mason.

Celtic Knot Maker

Paint breaks on a grid and the knotwork weaves itself, over and under, with George Bain's method.

Islamic Patterns

Stars, rosettes and interlaced girih grown from tilings of polygons with Hankin's method, saved as SVG or PNG.

Labyrinth Maker

Draw the Cretan labyrinth, a Troy Town or your own design from its path sequence, then walk it to the center.

Enso Maker

Paint the Zen circle in one breath: a generative ensō with dry-brush streaks on rice paper, or draw your own.

Divination

Ask a question and read the answer in numbers, runes, tarot cards or the hexagrams of the I Ching.

Motivational Numerology

Your core numbers from your name and birth date, with a short interpretation of each.

Tarot Reading

Shuffle the 78 cards of the tarot, lay out a spread and get the meaning of each card, upright or reversed.

I Ching Reading

Cast a hexagram with three coins and read its judgment, image and changing lines from the Book of Changes.

Rune Reading

Draw runes from the Elder Futhark and get their traditional meanings.

Sky and calendars

Follow the cycles of the sky: the phases of the Moon, the retrogrades of Mercury and the eclipses to come.

Mercury Retrograde

Is Mercury retrograde today? The stations and shadows, the loop it draws in the sky, and a calendar of every planet's retrogrades.

Moon Phase Calendar

Today's moon and month and year calendars of every phase, with moon signs, full moon names and what each phase means.

Eclipse Calendar

When is the next eclipse? Every solar and lunar eclipse from 1900 to 2100, where it is seen and what it looks like from your city.
