Evoluteur > Generative Art

Generative Art

Patterns that grow from a few rules, whether the rules come from chemistry, physics, algorithms or centuries of craft. Everything is computed live in your browser: tune the parameters, watch it change, and download the result.

Simulations

Nature's patterns from simple rules: vibrating sand, ice, currents, chaos, chemistry, the sunflower, a slime mold, living cells, coral and lightning, and flocks of birds.

Cymatics

Play a frequency and watch the sand settle into its Chladni figure, on a plate or a drumhead.

Snowflake Grower

Grow snow crystals on a hexagonal grid with Reiter's cellular automaton: set the humidity and frost to get dendrites, ferns, plates or lace.

Flow Fields

Thousands of particles follow Perlin noise and draw its invisible currents, like ink in water.

Strange Attractors

Draw the Lorenz butterfly and other strange attractors from tens of millions of points of their chaotic orbits, as glowing long exposures.

Reaction-Diffusion

Grow Turing patterns (spots, stripes, coral, worms, waves) from the Gray-Scott model in real time, paint with chemicals and download the pattern.

Sunflower Golden Angle

Grow a sunflower from the golden angle, nudge it a tenth of a degree to see spokes appear, and count its Fibonacci spirals.

Slime Mold

Thousands of agents follow each other's scent and grow the vein networks of the slime mold. Lay food with your finger.

Murmurations

Thousands of birds follow three simple rules and turn as one, like starlings at dusk. Your pointer is the falcon.

Game of Life

Conway's Game of Life and its cousins: gliders, guns, replicators, mazes and Brian's Brain. Draw cells or write your own rule.

Lightning and Coral

Particles wander and stick: coral, frost and crystals grow, and lightning steps down until it strikes the ground.

Curves and mazes

Paths drawn by algorithms, random tiles and pendulums.

Maze Maker

Square, circular, triangular and heart-shaped mazes from seven algorithms, to solve with the keyboard or a finger and export as SVG or PNG.

Labyrinth Maker

Draw the Cretan labyrinth, a Troy Town or your own design from its path sequence, then walk it to the center.

Harmonograph Maker

Musical intervals drawn by pendulums: harmonograph spirals and Lissajous curves for the octave.

Spiral Gallery

The logarithmic, Archimedean, Fermat and Fibonacci spirals side by side, with the nautilus, the ram's horn and the galaxy.

Truchet Tiles

One tile turned at random weaves into mazes, meanders and closed loops: Truchet's triangles, Smith's quarter arcs and the diagonals of 10 PRINT.

Space-Filling Curves

One line through every cell of a square: Hilbert, Peano, Gosper's flowsnake, the dragon and more, to download as SVG.

Fractals

Detail at every scale: coastlines measured with ever smaller rulers, a formula repeated, circles fitted into gaps.

Infinite Coastlines

An interactive guide to the coastline paradox and fractal dimension: shrinking rulers, the Koch snowflake and the Mandelbrot set.

Mandelbrot and Julia

Zoom into the Mandelbrot set, and see the Julia set of every point under the pointer.

Apollonian Gasket

Circles packed into circles forever, by Descartes' circle theorem, with whole-number curvatures.

Patterns from traditions

The rules of artisans, from Islamic tilings to Japanese lattices, rebuilt as generators.

Islamic Patterns

Stars, rosettes and interlaced girih grown from tilings of polygons with Hankin's method, saved as SVG or PNG.

Kolam Maker

South Indian dot-grid designs where a single unbroken line loops around every dot. Watch it being drawn, in one stroke.

Celtic Knot Maker

Paint breaks on a grid and the knotwork weaves itself, over and under, with George Bain's method.

Rose Window Maker

Build Gothic tracery with compass and straightedge, step by step, like a medieval mason.

Kumiko Maker

The Japanese wooden lattice: asanoha, goma and kikkō patterns, assembled piece by piece in a square, hexagonal or round frame.

Sashiko Maker

Japanese running-stitch patterns, white thread on indigo: seigaiha waves, asanoha, shippō and more, stitched out line by line.

Sacred geometry and Zen

Circles, triangles and symmetry, drawn to scale or by hand.

Sacred Geometry

Draw and export the Vesica Piscis, Seed of Life, Flower of Life, Metatron's Cube and the Golden Spiral.

Mandala Maker

Draw with mirrored symmetry, choose from 2 to 24 folds, and save your mandala as a PNG or an SVG.

Sri Yantra

The nine interlocking triangles with lotus petals and gates, drawn to scale, colorable and exportable as SVG or PNG.

Enso Maker

Paint the Zen circle in one breath: a generative ensō with dry-brush streaks on rice paper, or draw your own.

Zen Garden

A Zen rock garden: rake the sand, place and move the stones, and let the ripples form around them.
