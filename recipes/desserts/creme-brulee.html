Crème brûlée recipe

Prep Time: 25 minutes - Cook Time: 1+ hour - Servings: 6

Ingredients for 6 large or 10 small crèmes brûlées:

  • 45 cl heavy whipping cream (or half-and-half for the lighter version)
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 15 cl of milk (or almond milk)
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 150 g of white granulated sugar
  • Brown sugar
  • Vanilla extract
  • A few raspberries, or lemon zest, or banana chunks, or pineapple chunks (optional)
  • Rum or liqueur (optional)

Tools:

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • Ramekins
  • Torch or branding iron

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 250°F (120°C).
  2. Beat the egg yolks (save the whites for meringues).
  3. Add the sugar and a pinch of salt.
  4. Add the cream, the milk and the vanilla.
  5. Pour into the ramekins.
  6. (optional) Add fruit to some ramekins.
  7. Prepare "bain-marie": fill a long baking dish 1/3 with water and put the ramekins in it (the water should not go in the ramekins).
  8. Bake for about 1 hour depending on your oven (they are cooked when firm but not hard).
  9. Let cool down and put in the fridge for a few hours (they will be harder when cold).

Caramelizing the crust (just before serving):

    Using a torch (I personally use a Bernzomatic):
  1. Put some brown sugar on the cold custard.
  2. Point the flame at the brown sugar (not too close) until it melts into caramel.
    Using a crème brûlée branding iron (the old-fashioned way):
  1. Put some brown sugar on the cold custard.
  2. Heat the branding iron by putting it directly on the burner until it's hot (but not turning red).
  3. Put the iron on the brown sugar and it will turn into caramel.
  4. If the iron is hot enough, it can do 2 crèmes in a row; otherwise, reheat it on the burner before the next one.

Notes:

  • Great with a glass of Sauternes.
  • Replace the heavy whipping cream with half-and-half (and cook 10 minutes longer) for a lighter version.
